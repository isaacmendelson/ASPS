The origin

A phone call that shouldn't have worked.

By the founding team · Tel Aviv, Summer 2023

One of our co-founders' mothers received a call from her son. He was in trouble, he needed money, he sounded frightened. She nearly sent the transfer. The call was an AI clone — built from a 14-second clip of him on a podcast.

She was a careful person. A professional. She knew about scams. None of that mattered, because the attack didn't go through her judgement — it went through her ear, in her son's voice, in a moment of fear engineered to skip everything she knew.

We sat down a week later and tried to find a product we could buy her. Anti-virus didn't see it. Carriers didn't see it. Banks didn't see it. The whole defence stack was pointed at networks and accounts, and the attack had walked straight past it — through the human, in the moment.

So we built ASPS to sit exactly there. Not at the bank. Not at the carrier. On the person, in the moment.

What we hold to

Five principles, in writing.

Public commitments · Audited annually

  1. Explain every alert.

    If we can't tell a user — in one short sentence — why we flagged something, we don't flag it. No black box, no "trust the AI." Every verdict ships with its reason, plain language, on the same screen.

  2. The user keeps the final say.

    ASPS does not block, hang up or freeze on the user's behalf without confirmation. We don't replace human judgement — we restore the conditions for it.

  3. On-device first.

    Where the analysis can happen on the device, it does. Audio doesn't go to our servers. Verdicts and patterns do; the contents of conversations don't.

  4. No selling of behavioural data.

    Ever. Not for ads, not for "partner insights", not for any reason a marketing team might invent next quarter. We're paid by the people we protect.

  5. Audited by people we don't pay.

    Annual third-party audits — security, model behaviour, data flows. Findings published, not buried. If we ever skip one, the next signup screen has to say so.

Ethics & trust

Boring, on purpose.

We make decisions about other people's calls, messages and money. We treat that responsibility the way an old bank treats a vault — with paperwork, audits, and people whose job is to say no.

This page is the public-facing surface of that work. The full data policy, audit summaries and model cards live in the privacy and platform pages.

Commitments we publish.

  • Annual third-party audits — security, model behaviour, data flows.
  • Model cards for every production detector, refreshed each release.
  • Bias & accuracy reports per language, per region, per channel.
  • Data-residency guarantees in the EU, UK, Israel and select APAC regions.
  • Incident disclosure within 72 hours, regardless of jurisdiction.
  • No behavioural-data sales — written into our terms, not a marketing promise.
