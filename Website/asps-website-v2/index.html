The growing threat

A global epidemic targeting people — not systems.

Scams have become one of the fastest-growing global threats. Attackers no longer need to hack systems. They manipulate people directly using AI, voice cloning, and social engineering.

$442B Lost to scams annually Source: GASA 2025
57% Of adults targeted yearly More than 1 in 2 people
300% Rise in AI-powered scams Year over year
Zero Real-time solutions existed Until now
01 / Vector

AI-powered attacks

Voice cloning, deepfakes, and AI-generated content make scams more convincing than ever. Attackers can now mimic anyone — family members, bank employees, executives.

02 / Vector

Social engineering

Scammers exploit trust, fear, urgency, and loneliness. They manipulate emotions to bypass rational thinking and pressure victims into immediate action.

03 / Vector

Anyone is a target

No level of intelligence or technical knowledge guarantees protection. Doctors, lawyers, engineers, and cybersecurity experts have all fallen victim.

Why now?

Generative AI removed the last friction from scams. Voice cloning, deepfake video, and personalized phishing scaled overnight. Detection has to live at the moment of decision — too late once the call ends or the link is clicked.
