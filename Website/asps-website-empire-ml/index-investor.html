Mission

Our mission.

Three areas, treated as one problem: real-time protection, public conversation, and learning directly from the people who’ve been through it.

Technology

Real-time fraud protection at the moment of contact, across calls, messages, and browsers.

Public conversation

Surfacing fraud as a topic in households, workplaces, media and policy.

Lived experience

Interviews with people who’ve been targeted, fed back into product design, public education, and outreach to peer organizations.

Market

An emerging category.

Endpoint security solved a generation of machine-targeted threats. Human-targeted fraud is the next category — large, fragmented, and structurally underserved.

Why now

Generative AI removed the last friction from scams. Voice cloning, deepfake video and personalized phishing scaled overnight. Detection has to live at the moment of decision — too late once the call ends or the link is clicked.

Why us

30+ years building monitoring infrastructure, plus a team with offensive and defensive cyber backgrounds. Founders who studied how scammers operate and built systems that watch every channel as one.
