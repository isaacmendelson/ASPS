Why this exists

Every story you tell us teaches the next alert.

Each story we receive — the cold call, the impossible voice, the bank tab that wasn't quite right — becomes a pattern. The pattern becomes an alert. The alert reaches someone, in time, who would otherwise have spent thirty seconds too long believing what they were told.

We do not romanticise this. People lose more than money to scams; they lose trust in their own judgement. Telling us, even anonymously, is one way to take some of that back — and to make sure the next person does not have to discover the same trick the same way.

From submit to alert

What happens to your story, step by step.

01

Personal details are scrubbed before review.

Names, phone numbers, account numbers, amounts and addresses are automatically replaced with neutral tokens (NAME_01, AMOUNT_01) before anyone sees the story. The original is encrypted and accessible only to you via your withdraw link.

Within 60 secondsAutomatic

02

A human reads it. A person, not a queue.

Our care team — three people, all of whom have lived or family experience of scams — read every story within five working days. They tag it for pattern detection and reply to you, in your language.

≤ 5 working daysCare team

03

Patterns inform the next alert template.

If your story includes a script, number or hook we haven't seen, the detection team writes a new alert rule. Most rules ship to all users within 7–14 days of the story arriving. You'll be told if yours did.

7–14 daysDetection

04

If we want to publish, you see it first.

For stories with permission to publish, we send the proposed version to you before it goes anywhere. You can edit a single word, edit everything, or pull it entirely. The default is anonymous; even attribution is opt-in per publication.

Before publicAlways reviewed

05

You can withdraw, forever.

The link in your confirmation email never expires. One click and your story — and every derived alert that quotes you directly — is purged. Patterns learned from your story stay; quotes do not.

Anytime · foreverOne click
