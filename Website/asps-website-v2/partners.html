The case

The cost of catching it late.

Operational risk · Regulatory exposure · Trust

Most fraud tooling watches for the transaction. By then, the conversation that authorised it ended an hour ago — and under new reimbursement rules, the loss lands on you.

The attacker no longer needs to break a system. They convince a person. That person calls your contact centre after the wire, not before, and the regulator now expects you to have stopped it.

Detection at the moment of the call, the message or the click moves the cost of the scam out of operations and out of the P&L.

ASPS is the layer between your customer and the social-engineering attack — explainable, real-time, and built to slot inside your existing fraud stack rather than replace it.

Deployment

From contract to first protected customer in 90 days.

A phased rollout designed to slot inside existing risk, security and product workflows — not run alongside them.

  1. Scope

    Weeks 1–2

    A short discovery sprint with your fraud, product and risk teams. We map the scam vectors hitting you most, the channels where it lands, and the deployment surface that makes sense first.

    • Threat-vector analysis on your real loss data
    • Architecture review with your security team
    • Success criteria and KPI definition signed off

  2. Integrate

    Weeks 3–8

    Connect via REST API, mobile SDK, or our white-label app. Sandbox first, then staging — your engineers stay in control, we stay on the line.

    • API / SDK / white-label, your choice
    • Single-tenant data plane in your region
    • Dedicated solutions engineer through go-live

  3. Pilot

    Weeks 9–12

    Live with a controlled cohort. We tune thresholds against your customers' behaviour, hand back daily intelligence reports, and prove the loss reduction in your own numbers.

    • Opt-in customer cohort, real traffic
    • Weekly review with fraud ops
    • Loss reduction measured in £ / $ / ₪ saved

  4. Scale

    From week 13

    Expand to your full base on your schedule — with ongoing intelligence from the broader ASPS network feeding back into your detection model.

    • Full base rollout, channel by channel
    • Quarterly business reviews and roadmap input
    • Shared intelligence across the partner network
