Everything you need to know about ASPS.

ASPS is a full-envelope scam protection system. It monitors calls, texts, emails, and browser activity in real time and uses AI-powered analysis to detect threats before you act. The protection layer runs on your device. The intelligence behind it is AI.
ASPS runs on Mac, Android, and iOS. On desktop, it also includes a browser extension that monitors your web activity in real time. Each device runs its own app and they all work together as one unified protection layer around you.
Scammers rarely use one channel. They'll call you while sending a link over WhatsApp. Or email you while impersonating your bank on screen. ASPS sees all your devices as one system and detects patterns across them simultaneously. The moment something looks coordinated and suspicious, you're alerted before you act.
Think of it this way: antivirus is ASPS for computers. ASPS is antivirus for people.

Antivirus blocks malicious code. ASPS blocks manipulation the social engineering that tricks real humans into handing over money or trust voluntarily. No existing software was built to stop that. We were.
