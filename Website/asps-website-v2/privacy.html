Privacy policy.

Edition3.1 Effective12 May 2026 Last auditMar 2026 · BDO ControllerASPS Ltd · Tel Aviv
§ 01

What this policy covers.

This policy applies to every product and service ASPS Ltd ("we", "ASPS") offers — the mobile apps, the browser extensions, the family-loop service, the partner deployments and this website. If your relationship with ASPS is through a bank or telco, their privacy policy also applies, but never replaces the protections below.

Written in plain English. The legal-counsel version lives in the appendix and is identical in effect.

The shortest possible answer: pattern data and verdicts, yes; the contents of your conversations, no. The longer version, by category:

§ 03

Why we hold what we hold.

Three reasons, and only three:

  • To run the service you signed up for — show alerts, sync across devices, bill you for the plan you chose.
  • To improve detection — pattern data (not your content) makes the model better at catching the next call.
  • To comply with applicable law — tax records, fraud-prevention obligations, lawfully valid orders.

That's the entire list. We don't hold data for marketing, partner enrichment, or speculative future products.

§ 04

Where your data lives.

Your data is pinned to the region you signed up in. Practically:

  • EU users — stored in our Frankfurt and Dublin regions. Never leaves the EU outside lawful transfer scenarios with appropriate safeguards.
  • UK users — stored in London. Same rules under the UK GDPR.
  • Israeli users — stored in Tel Aviv, subject to the Privacy Protection Law.
  • APAC users — stored in Singapore or Tokyo depending on jurisdiction.

The full data-residency map, including which regional Cloud provider holds which slice, is published on the ethics page.

§ 05

Your rights, in writing.

Under GDPR, UK GDPR and equivalent laws, you have the right to:

  • Access everything we hold about you (Account → Export).
  • Correct anything inaccurate.
  • Delete everything we hold (Account → Delete).
  • Object to specific processing, including pattern-data contribution.
  • Take it elsewhere — portable JSON, no friction.
  • Complain to your DPA — we'll never make you go through us first.

In short

You can export everything, delete everything, or pause data collection — from the same screen, in your account. We don't gatekeep your data behind a support ticket.

§ 06

Who we share it with — and who we don't.

Our service runs on a handful of vendors: cloud infrastructure, billing, email delivery, security monitoring. Each one is bound by a DPA, audited annually, and listed by name on the sub-processor page (linked in your account settings).

We do not share, sell, license, swap or trade your data with advertisers, data brokers, marketing networks, "partner" insights programmes, or anyone whose business model is data.

§ 07

Government & legal requests.

We respond only to lawfully valid requests in the relevant jurisdiction. We push back on overbroad ones. We publish a transparency report every six months listing the number, scope and outcome of requests.

Because most of your content never leaves your device, the surface area available to a compelled request is small by design — not by accident.

§ 08

If something goes wrong.

We commit to disclosing material incidents that affect user data within 72 hours of confirmed scope, regardless of jurisdictional minimums. Disclosure is sent by email and posted on the trust page; if we ever miss a deadline, that fact is itself disclosed.

For routine privacy questions, write to privacy@asps.security. Our Data Protection Officer reads every message.

Terms of service.

Edition3.1 Effective12 May 2026 Governing lawIsrael · with EU consumer-rights add-on
§ 01

What you get, what we promise.

You get access to the ASPS platform — alerts, dashboards, family-loop, the features described on your plan. We promise to keep them running, keep them honest, and tell you when something changes.

Service-level commitments (uptime, alert latency, support response times) are published per plan on the pricing page.

§ 02

What we ask of you.

  • Use it for yourself — or for people who have consented (e.g. family members).
  • Don't try to break it — exploits should go through responsible disclosure, not production.
  • Don't resell or wrap it — without an enterprise agreement (which we're happy to talk about).
  • Be over 16, or have a parent set it up for you.
§ 03

Honest limits of what software can do.

ASPS detects patterns; it does not guarantee outcomes. No detector is 100%, and scams evolve. We do everything we can to catch them; you remain responsible for your final decisions.

Where ASPS issues an alert that you confirm and act on, and the alert turns out to be wrong, our false-positive credit may apply — see your plan's terms.

§ 04

Money, refunds, cancellation.

  • Monthly billing, prorated to the day on upgrade or downgrade.
  • Cancel anytime from the app — no retention calls, no exit fees.
  • EU consumer-rights withdrawal: 14 days, full refund, no questions.
  • Annual plans: prorated refund on cancellation, minus services rendered.
§ 05

If we end it, or you end it.

You can leave at any time. We can terminate accounts only for clearly listed reasons (abuse, fraud, sustained non-payment) and with reasonable notice except in safety cases. Your data export remains available for 30 days after termination.

§ 06

Disputes & the grown-up section.

For EU/UK consumers, the consumer protection regime in your country always applies and overrides any choice-of-law clause that would weaken it. For everyone else, Israeli law governs and Tel Aviv courts have jurisdiction.

Before anything escalates, we promise a real human reply to any complaint within 5 working days. Most things end there.

§ 01

What we set, and what we don't.

This website sets only the cookies it needs to remember your language, your consent choices and your session. We do not use third-party advertising, social or tracking cookies — including Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight, or any equivalent.

The mobile apps don't set cookies in the web sense; the data they hold is described in the privacy policy above.

§ 03

Analytics — privacy-respecting.

We use a self-hosted analytics tool (Plausible, EU-hosted) that does not set cookies and does not fingerprint visitors. It tells us "X pages were viewed today from the UK" — not "this person from this IP did these things". The full configuration is published in our open-source repo.

§ 04

How to turn things off.

Every browser lets you clear or block cookies for any site — including this one. Doing so will mean you have to re-choose your language and log in again on each visit, but the site otherwise works fine. We do not, and will not, gate content behind cookie acceptance.

If anything on this page is unclear, write to privacy@asps.security. A human reads every message — usually back to you within 24 hours.
