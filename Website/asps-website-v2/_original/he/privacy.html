מדיניות פרטיות

How we handle your data clearly and honestly.

Effective Date: February 26, 2026

1. Who We Are

ASPS (Anti-Scam Protection Systems) is a US-based company building consumer scam protection technology. Our mission is to give every person access to the kind of real-time fraud protection that was previously only available to enterprises.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, you can reach us at: info@asps.io

2. What We Collect

At this pre-launch stage, we collect only what is necessary to operate our waitlist and respond to inquiries:

  • Email address submitted voluntarily when you join our waitlist.
  • Contact form submissions your name, email address, and message if you reach out through our contact form.
  • Usage data basic browser data (cookies and local storage) used solely for site functionality. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

We do not collect financial data, government IDs, or any sensitive personal information.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes only:

  • To send you waitlist updates and product launch notifications.
  • To respond to your inquiries submitted via the contact form.
  • To understand how visitors interact with our site and improve it over time.
🔒 We do not sell your data. We do not share your email or personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for commercial purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We keep your data as close as possible. The only third-party service that may receive your personal data is:

  • Formspree (formspree.io) used to process contact form submissions. Formspree's own מדיניות פרטיות governs how they handle data submitted through their service.

No other third parties receive your personal data. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking services.

5. Data Retention

  • Waitlist emails retained until you unsubscribe or until the ASPS product officially launches, whichever comes first.
  • Contact form submissions retained for up to 12 months from the date of submission, then deleted.

You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 6).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@asps.io with the subject line "Data Request."

  • Right to access Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to deletion Ask us to delete your data. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Right to opt out Unsubscribe from waitlist emails at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email we send.

7. Cookies

We use minimal functional cookies only small files stored in your browser that help the site work correctly (e.g., remembering your preferences).

  • We do not use tracking cookies.
  • We do not use advertising cookies.
  • We do not use analytics cookies from third-party platforms.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. This will not affect your ability to browse our site.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us at info@asps.io and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our product evolves. When we make material changes, we will notify waitlist members via email and update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out:

  • Email: info@asps.io
  • Company: ASPS (Anti-Scam Protection Systems)
  • Location: United States

We are committed to responding to all data-related requests within 30 days.
