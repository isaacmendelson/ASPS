Protecting people from manipulation.

We're building technology that helps people recognize and resist scams — not after the damage, but in the moment it matters most.

המשימה שלנו

Real-time protection for everyone.

Scams are a global epidemic affecting billions of people. Yet until now, there has been no real solution that protects people during the scam itself.

Traditional anti-fraud tools focus on protecting systems — detecting fraudulent transactions, blocking suspicious emails, filtering spam. But modern scams don't attack systems. They attack people.

Scammers use psychology, urgency, fear, and increasingly AI to manipulate victims into taking actions they wouldn't otherwise take. By the time a transaction is flagged, the damage is often done.

ASPS is different. We protect people at the moment of decision — when they're on a call with a scammer, when they're about to click a malicious link, when they're being manipulated in real-time. We give people the information they need to make the right choice.

אתיקה ואמון

Our ethical commitments.

Building protection technology comes with significant responsibility. Here's how we hold ourselves accountable.

  • 1

    Transparency in detection

    When we flag something as a potential scam, we explain why. Users should understand our reasoning and be empowered to make their own decisions.

  • 2

    No exploitation of fear

    We will never use fear-based marketing or exaggerate threats to drive adoption. Protection should empower, not frighten.

  • 3

    Data minimization

    We collect the minimum data necessary to provide protection. What we don't collect, we can't lose or misuse.

  • 4

    No surveillance capitalism

    We will never sell user data or use it for advertising. Our business model is protection, not surveillance.

  • 5

    Bias vigilance

    We actively work to identify and eliminate bias in our detection systems. Protection must work equally well for everyone.

Our commitment to responsible AI

We recognize that AI is a powerful tool that can be used for both protection and manipulation. We commit to using AI only for protection, with full transparency about how our systems work.

We believe that the same technologies being used by scammers — voice cloning, deepfakes, AI-generated content — can and should be used to protect people. But this requires constant vigilance and ethical reflection.

"Technology should protect human agency, not undermine it. Every system we build is designed to help people make better decisions — never to make decisions for them."

Our story

How we got here.

ASPS started with a simple observation: scams are exploding, but protection hasn't kept up.

The problem

We saw friends and family fall victim to scams — smart, careful people manipulated by increasingly sophisticated attacks. Traditional solutions weren't working.

The insight

We realized that scams succeed not because people are foolish, but because attackers exploit universal human psychology. Protection needs to work in real-time, at the moment of manipulation.

The solution

We built ASPS to be the protection layer that didn't exist — analyzing communications in real-time, detecting manipulation tactics, and warning users before they make irreversible decisions.

The movement

Beyond technology, we're building a movement. Breaking the stigma around scams, sharing stories openly, and creating a world where being targeted is no longer shameful.
