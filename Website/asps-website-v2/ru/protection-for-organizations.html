Часто задаваемые вопросы

Will employees feel like we’re surveilling them?

No. ASPS processes signals — never the content of conversations — and the employer never sees individual activity. Enrollment is opt‑in and revocable at any time. The benefit lives on the employee’s device, not in your HR system.

Can we extend coverage to spouses, kids or elderly parents?

Yes. Family extensions are the most‑used feature. Most scams targeting your employees actually hit a parent or partner first — covering them closes the loop.

How is this priced?

Per protected seat, with volume tiers and discounted bundles for family extensions. We’ll quote on the call once we understand your headcount.

Do we need an SDK or integration?

Not for the benefit model. Employees install ASPS on their own devices and the rollup dashboard is hosted. For institutions that want native integration — banks, telecoms — we offer SDK and API paths too.

What about compliance and data residency?

ASPS supports regional data residency, SOC 2 controls and audit‑grade reporting. The full security & compliance package is available under NDA on request.
