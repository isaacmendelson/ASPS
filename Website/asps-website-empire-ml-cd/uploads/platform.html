How it works

Four steps to real-time protection.

From detection to protection in milliseconds — here's how ASPS keeps you safe.

1

Detect

ASPS continuously monitors your communications for signs of scam activity. Using AI trained on millions of real scam examples, we identify patterns that humans often miss.

  • Voice pattern analysis for AI cloning
  • Behavioral cues of social engineering
  • URL and domain reputation checking
  • Sender authentication verification
🔍
2

Analyze

When potential threats are detected, our AI analyzes context, intent, and risk level. We don't just flag keywords — we understand the full picture of what's happening.

  • Contextual understanding of conversations
  • Risk scoring based on multiple factors
  • Cross-reference with known scam patterns
  • Real-time threat intelligence
🧠
3

Alert

When a scam is detected, you receive an instant, clear warning. We explain exactly why we think something is suspicious, empowering you to make an informed decision.

  • Instant notifications when threats detected
  • Clear explanations of risk factors
  • Non-intrusive, actionable warnings
  • Customizable alert preferences
⚡
4

Protect

With the right information at the right time, you can make the right decision. ASPS helps you avoid financial loss, emotional harm, and the aftermath of being scammed.

  • One-tap actions to end suspicious calls
  • Safe verification options
  • Incident reporting and documentation
  • Ongoing protection and learning
