If it's happening now

The next five minutes matter most.

Do these in order. Don't worry about anything else yet — write to us afterwards, once you and your money are safe.

  1. 01

    Hang up. Don't move money.

    Every scam relies on you staying on the line and acting fast. Ending the call costs nothing and breaks the script. No bank, no police force and no relative will ever lose trust because you called them back.

  2. 02

    Call your bank from the number on the back of your card.

    Not the number the caller gave you. Not a number from a recent SMS. The card itself. Tell them you may have been targeted; they have a fraud team trained for this exact moment.

    UK159Stop Scams direct line IL105Police cyber unit EU116 006Victim support
  3. 03

    Change the password on your email first.

    Email is the master key. If a scammer is into your email, they can reset every other account. Do this before changing anything else.

  4. 04

    File a report — even if no money moved.

    It creates the paper trail you'll need for reimbursement, and it feeds the patterns the rest of us are tracking. UK: Action Fraud (0300 123 2040). Israel: police 105. EU: your national fraud authority.

  5. 05

    Then — and only then — write to us.

    Send the script, the number, the timing, anything you remember. It goes into our threat-pattern database. The next person who hears the same opening line will get a warning because of you.
