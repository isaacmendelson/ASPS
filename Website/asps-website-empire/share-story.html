Breaking the stigma

Let's destroy these myths.

The shame around scams is built on false beliefs. Here's the truth.

Myth

"Only naive or uneducated people fall for scams."

Truth

Scammers specifically target successful, educated people because they have more to lose. Intelligence doesn't protect you — awareness does.

Myth

"I should have known better."

Truth

Scammers are professional manipulators. They create scenarios designed to bypass rational thinking. Hindsight isn't a fair judge of decisions made under manufactured pressure.

Myth

"If I tell people, they'll think less of me."

Truth

Most people respond with empathy, not judgment. And those who judge often do so out of fear — a fear that diminishes when they hear your story.

Myth

"My story isn't important enough to share."

Truth

Every scam attempt — whether you lost money or not — contains valuable information. Your story might be the exact warning someone else needs to hear.
